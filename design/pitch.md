# dclplace

**One shared 320×320 canvas. One pixel every second. Nothing ever resets.**

Live at [dclplace.dcl.eth](https://decentraland.org/play/?realm=dclplace.dcl.eth) and Genesis Plaza South ([-9,-91](https://play.decentraland.org/?NETWORK=mainnet&position=-9,-91)). Shipped Sep 2026 for the Friendzone Mobile Buildathon.

**10 days in, zero paid marketing:**

- **62.5% D7 retention** — over 3× Decentraland's 20% program benchmark
- **19.2 min average session** — 98% of it active (0.34 min AFK)
- **74.29% of weekly visitors chat or emote**
- 184 unique visitors, peak 10 concurrent — growing organically from creator + user posts on Discord and Twitter

Players screenshot their work and share it. Those posts drive the next wave of visitors. The loop already works — v2 is designed to industrialize it.

**v2 in 4 weeks:**

1. **Private boards** — each player gets their own persistent canvas that overlays the public one. Practice, ambition, a space you own.
2. **Full color picker** — the 8-color quick-bar stays for coordination; the picker unlocks the rest for artists.
3. **One-tap image export** — the half-built server-side snapshot pipeline becomes a user-facing button. Screenshot-and-share becomes a one-tap flow.
4. **Board sharing** — send a link, someone loads your studio, views it. (Collaborative editing = v3.)

**Why it works:** the same growth channel that drove v1's numbers *is* the channel v2 amplifies. Not a new hypothesis — an existing loop, faster.
